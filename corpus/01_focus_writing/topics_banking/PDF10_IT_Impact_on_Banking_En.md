Impacts of IT on the Banking Sector of Bangladesh
“Technological innovations will be the ‘heart and blood’ of the banking industry for many years to come and if big banks
 do not make the most of it, the new players from FinTech and large technology companies surely will.” - David Brear

Banking is the backbone of the economy for any country.Technology has become a tool that facilitates banks' organizational
structures, business strategies, customer services and related functions.

Information Technology (IT):
Information Technology (IT) can be defined as the modern handling of information by electronic means, which involves
its access, storage, processing, transportation or transfer and delivery. In the financial institutions IT has been playing
enabling role in improving services, introducing new products, easy enquiry, and saving time.
Technology Driven Banking Services:
“Financial institutions must be able to deliver an easy to navigate, a seamless digital platform that goes far beyond a
miniaturized online banking offering.” -Jim marous
Information and communication technology (ICT) has become the heart of the banking sector in Bangladesh now-a-days.
Information technology plays two important roles in the banking:
    •   supportive role, and
    •   strategic role.
The supportive role helps banks to prepare the platform for business process reengineering and IT based financial
products.All banks in Bangladesh have made substantial investments in following technology driven services:

    ➢    Core Banking
    ➢    Internet Banking
    ➢    Mobile Banking
    ➢    SMS Banking
    ➢    Electronic Funds Transfer
    ➢    Any Branch Banking
    ➢    Automated Teller Machine (ATM)
    ➢    Point of Sale (POS)
    ➢    Debit Cards
    ➢    Credit Cards
    ➢    Banking KIOSK
    ➢    SWIFT
    ➢    MICR
    ➢    Open online Letter of Credit
    ➢    VISA/MasterCard
    ➢    Contact/Call Centre
    ➢    Automated Clearing House System (ACHS)
    ➢    Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT)
    ➢    Online CIB
    ➢    Corporate Intranet System and so on.

## পৃষ্ঠা 2

The usage of ICT in Bangladesh's banking industry:
According to Horseman & Michael,
Banks have adopted IT throughout the world for three basic reasons:
• To protect and increase market share
• To reduce operating costs
• To generate new revenue.
The usage of ICT in Bangladesh's banking industry began gradually in 1990, according to research by the Bangladesh
Institute of Bank Management (BIBM). The government’s initiatives to digitalize the financial service sector such as
establishing electronic platforms like NPSB (National Payment Switch Bangladesh) and BEFTN (Bangladesh Electronic
Funds Transfer Network) are playing a key role in the rapid progress of internet banking.
According to a research report by the Bangladesh Institute of Bank Management (BIBM), transactions through
traditional banking are now averaging 170 crore times in a year and due to ICT, more than 200 crore transactions are being
done through online. With the increasing use of technology, the efficiency of the bank's staff has become more than doubled
in the last decade and a half.
The advantages of using Information Technology in Banking: It has been said,
           “Technology is a gift of God. After the gift of life it is perhaps the greatest of God's gifts”
The bank sector verifies the authenticity of the above statement.A certain number of advantages of using Information
Technology in banking are:
   ➢ Self-inquiry facility: Facility for logging into specified self-inquiry terminals at the branch to inquire and view the
       transactions in the account.
   ➢ Remote banking: Remote terminals at the customer site connected to the respective branch through a modem,
       enabling the customer to make inquiries regarding his accounts, on-line, without having to move from his office.
   ➢ Anytime banking-Anywhere banking: ATMs which offer non-stop cash withdrawal, and inquiry facilities.
       Networking of computerized branches inter-city and intra-city, will permit customers of these branches, when
       interconnected, to transact from any of these branches.
   ➢ Telebanking: A 24-hour service through which inquiries regarding balances and transactions in the account can
       be made over the phone.

Impact of IT in Banking Sector:
To the Bank:
The major advantages for the bank to implement IT are:
    i)       Availability of a wide range of inquiry facilities
    ii)      Helping the bank in business development.
    iii)     Immediate replies to customer queries.
    iv)      Generation of various reports and periodical returns on due dates.
    v)       Fast and up-to-date information transfer enabling speedier decisions, by interconnecting computerized
             branches and controlling offices.
    vi)      Reduce cost by improving the efficiency of employees by automate business processes.
    vii)     Innovative, scheme, addresses competition and present the bank as technology driven in the banking sector
             market
    viii)    Reduces customer visits to the branch and thereby human intervention
    ix)      Inter-branch reconciliation
    x)       Reducing chances of fraud and misappropriation
    xi)      Integrated customer data paves way for individualized and customized services.
To the Employees:
IT has increased their productivity through the followings:
                i) Accurate computing of cumbersome and time-consuming jobs such as balancing and interest
                     calculations on due dates.
                ii) Automatic printing of covering schedules, deposit receipts, pass book / pass sheet (transaction
                     documents), freeing the staff from performing these time-consuming jobs, and enabling them to give
                     more attention to the needs of the customer.
                iii) Signature retrieval facility, assisting in verification of transactions, sitting at their own terminal.
                iv) Avoidance of duplication of entries due to existence of single-point data entry.
                v) Anywhere Banking no matter wherever the customer is in the world. Balance enquiry, request for
                     services, issuing instructions etc., from anywhere in the world is possible.
                vi) Anytime Banking — managing funds in real time and most importantly, 24 hours a day, 7days a week.
                vii) Brings down “Cost of Banking” to the customer over a period a period of time.
                viii)     Cash withdrawal from any branch / ATM

Factors Affecting Staff’s Attitude Towards Banking Technology:
There are various factors affecting the attitude of bank staffs towards the application of banking information technologies:
    1. Fear of New Responsibilities
    2. Fear of Job Losses
    3. Fear of Losing Customer Relationship
    4. Lack of Strategic Information Technology Plan
    5. Lack of Training
    6. Compatibility (System Integration Problem)
    7. Complexity (System Technical Difficulties)
    8. Confidentiality And Privacy
    9. Lack of Appropriate Technical Knowledge Amongst Bank Staff

At corporate level to meet the challenges, various initiated have been taken and implementation is process beside up
gradation of data centre facilities:
    • Centralization of functions
    • Inward clearing data uploading and processing
    • Check book issues
    • On-Line Monitoring/ Generation of statement by controlling offices
    • Audit from the remote location
    • Sending mails and statement of accounts to customers & completion of non-mandatory field in newly opened
        accounts.
    • Call Centre for customers.
    • Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Application.
    • Data Warehousing.
    • Customer / Account data completion / correction/Customer-ID crystallization.
    • Aggressive marketing of Internet Banking & Debit Card products to increase share of delivery channels transaction.
    • Skill up gradation & increase in awareness of all staff member.
    • Messages issued through scrolling ticker on login page.
    Recommendations:
   •   Fortifying ICT department through providing training to IT employees and procuring essential hardware and
       software.
   •   Creating separate unit in each branch for rendering ICT related operational activities under the supervision of the
       ICT department.
   •   To train all staff about the basic ICT related matters.
   •   Adequate legal and environmental framework is essential for flourishing tech-based banking.
   •   Technology driven banking can be promoted through advertising in print and electronic media.
   •   The banks can give few incentives to the customers for using the technology driven services such as low service
       charge for maintaining the account of those customers.
   •   The tech-based banking products can be made more cost effective to make them more attractive to mass people.
   •   In order to detect and minimize the security risk the banks can take some security counter measures, such as,
       preventive measures or deterrent efforts.
   •   The government may provide assistance for reducing the cost of providing the technology driven banking
       services, organize training facilities with private partnership, and widen multiple access facilities like web,
       telephone, ATM etc.

Conclusion:

                “Banks have to upgrade themselves, or risk being burnt to the ground.” – JP Nicols

Banking Sector has become the ‘Lifeline’ of economy wheareas IT has become the ‘Oxygen’ of this sector. Banks have
contributed considerably in the process of economic growth of Bangladesh. However, their contribution would have been
greater, if they had effectively addressed various IT related challenges. To spur innovation, productivity and growth of
economy, the banking system needs upgradation and urgent measures are needed for rapid expansion of technology driven
banking in the country. Government as well as the concerned authority should take necessary and timely action to implement
an IT based sound banking sector for the sustainable development of our economy.
