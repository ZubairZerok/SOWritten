“Mobile Financial Services (MFS) is an approach to offering financial services that combines banking with mobile
 wireless networks which enables users to execute banking transactions.”—Bangladesh Bank

Mobile banking is a Mobile Financial service (MFS) provided by a bank or other financial institution that allows
its customers to conduct financial transactions remotely using a mobile device such as a smartphone or tablet.

Challenges Associated with Mobile Banking:
Some of the challenges associated with mobile banking include (but are not limited to):
Mobile Banking causes inconvenience for less tech-savvy account holders.
Raises security concerns and online fraud, susceptible to hacking and other cyber security threats.
It can be less secure than traditional banking, as mobile devices are often lost or stolen.
It can be expensive, as customers may need to pay for data plans or other fees to use the service.
Results in delays or losses in transactions due to mistakes.
Gives rise to comprehension issues due to the complex app interface.
Delays service requests in case of internet issues.
It can be intrusive, as some banks may send customers marketing messages or other information that
they do not want to receive.
