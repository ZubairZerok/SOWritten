Ans: "Fossil fuels" refer to non-renewable energy sources, such as coal, oil, and natural gas that
are formed from the remains of ancient plants and animals buried and subjected to heat and
pressure over millions of years.

What is the relationship between climate change and fossil fuels?

Ans: The burning of fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide (CO2) and other greenhouse gases into
the atmosphere. These gases trap heat, leading to the warming of the Earth's surface and
contributing to climate change. The increased concentration of greenhouse gases, primarily from
the combustion of fossil fuels, is a significant driver of global warming and climate-related
challenges.

How is artificial intelligence (AI) contributing to the Fourth Industrial Revolution, and which
sectors are particularly affected by its advancements?

Ans: Artificial intelligence (AI) is a central component of the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR),
contributing to unprecedented advancements in technology. It plays a pivotal role in reshaping
industries such as healthcare, logistics, manufacturing, and customer service by automating
processes, enhancing efficiency, and enabling new capabilities. The integration of AI into
various sectors is transforming traditional practices and fostering innovation in areas like
personalized medicine, smart manufacturing, and intelligent data analysis.

In which ways does the Internet of Things (IoT) play a pivotal role in the 4IR, and how does it
impact manufacturing processes, healthcare delivery, and urban infrastructure?

Ans: The Internet of Things (IoT) is crucial in the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR) by
connecting physical devices and enabling seamless data exchange. In manufacturing, IoT
facilitates real-time monitoring and control, optimizing production processes. In healthcare, IoT
supports remote patient monitoring and smart medical devices, enhancing healthcare delivery.
