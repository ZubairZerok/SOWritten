1.   Read the passage and answer the questions (i-vi)                                               [5*6=30]
     The Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR) marks a paradigm shift in the way we perceive and engage
     with technology, redefining the landscape of industries and societies worldwide. Building upon the
     foundations of its predecessors, the 4IR is characterized by the convergence of digital, physical, and
     biological technologies, heralding a new era of innovation and transformation. At the heart of the 4IR
     lies the unprecedented advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning.
     The Internet of Things (IoT) plays a pivotal role in the 4IR by connecting physical devices and
     enabling seamless data exchange. From smart homes and wearable to industrial sensors, the IoT
     creates interconnected ecosystems that facilitate real-time monitoring and control. The integration of
     robotics with AI is reshaping industries and the workforce alike. Robots are no longer confined to
     repetitive tasks on factory floors; they are increasingly becoming integral to healthcare, logistics, and
     customer service. Collaborative robots, or cobots, work alongside human counterparts, augmenting
     capabilities and improving overall productivity. The evolution of robotics in the 4IR is not just about
     automation but also about creating new opportunities for human-robot collaboration.
     Biotechnology and genomics are driving scientific breakthroughs in the 4IR, offering solutions to
     healthcare and agriculture. Advances in gene editing technologies, such as CRISPR-Cas9, are paving
     the way for personalized medicine tailored to an individual's genetic makeup. In agriculture, genomics
     is enhancing crop yield and resilience, contributing to sustainable and efficient food production.
     Renewable energy and sustainability are central to the 4IR's vision for a greener future. Technologies
     like solar power, wind energy, and energy storage solutions are reducing dependence on fossil fuels.
     Smart grids and energy-efficient technologies are optimizing energy distribution and consumption,
     aligning with global efforts to combat climate change.
     The Fourth Industrial Revolution is a transformative force, reshaping industries and societies through
     the integration of advanced technologies. While presenting unprecedented opportunities for innovation
     and efficiency, the 4IR also brings forth challenges such as job displacement, ethical considerations,
     and the need for comprehensive regulatory frameworks.
     Questions:
        i. What do you understand by‘fossil fuels’
       ii. What is the relationship between climate change and fossil fuels?
      iii. How is artificial intelligence (AI) contributing to the Fourth Industrial Revolution, and which

      iv. In which ways does the Internet of Things (IoT) play a pivotal role in the 4IR, and how does it
             impact manufacturing processes, healthcare delivery, and urban infrastructure?
       v. What are the challenges of 4IR? What is your suggestion to harness the full potential of the
             4IR?
      vi. Choose a suitable title for the above comprehension.
