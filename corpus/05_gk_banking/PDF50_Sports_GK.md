The Ashes represents a biennial Test cricket showdown between England and Australia. The name 'Ashes' found
its origins when England suffered its first-ever home soil defeat to Australia at The Oval on August 29, 1882. A
classic Ashes series comprises five Tests, taking turns to be hosted by England and Australia, with a minimum
two-year gap. If the series is drawn, the team that currently holds the Ashes "retains" the trophy.
      Administrator: International Cricket Council (ICC)
