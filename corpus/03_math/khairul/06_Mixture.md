Khairul’s Bank Written Math                    410                                   Mixture

                                       Mixture

General problems:
1. 100 kg of solution A is mixed with 60 kg of solution B. If solution A has tin and copper
   in the ratio 1 : 4 and solution B has lead and tin in the ratio 3 : 2, then what is the
   amount of tin in the new solution ? [UCBL (AO)-2018 (Written)]                  Ans: 44 kg
2. A trader purchases blue paint and yellow paint at a price of Tk. 110 and Tk. 100 per
   liter respectively. He mixes blue and yellow paint at a ratio of 1 : 2 to make a certain
   paint of green color. He sells the green paint at a price of Tk. 120 per liter. If the trader
   makes a profit of Tk. 2000, how much yellow paint (in kgs) did he use in the mixture?
   [DBBL-(PO)-2017(Written)]                                                    Ans: 80 liters.
3. A gasoline company wants to provide a customer with 1000 liters of premium gasoline
   Tk. 60 per liter by mixing X liter of regular gasoline costing Tk. 50 per liter, with Y
   liters of unleaded gasoline costing Tk.66 per liter. How much of each gasoline should be
   used to produce the mixture? [Mercantile Bank (PO)-2011- (Written)]
                                                                  Ans: 375 liters & 625 liters.
4. A trader mixes 26 kg of rice priced at Tk. 20 per kg with 30 kg rice of another variety
   priced at Tk. 36 per kg and sells the mixture at Tk. 30 per kg. What is the percentage of
   profit or loss on this sale? [DBBL (PO)2012 (Written)]                               Ans: 5%

Ratio Related Mixture:
5. Two equal glasses are respectively 1/3 and 1/4 full of milk. They are then filled up with
   water and the contents mixed in a tumbler. What is the ratio of milk and water in the
   tumbler? [Social Islami Bank (PO)-2013 (Written)]                              Ans: 7:17
 Self Task:
6. The ratio of milk and water in a mixture of 84 liters is 3 : 4. What will be the new ratio
   if 3 liters each of milk and water is added to the mixture?[Bangladesh Krishi Bank (SO)
   Examination –2011- (Written)]                                                  Ans: 13:17

7. A drink contains 20% mango juice, 20% guava juice and 60% apple juice. You added
   250ml of water to 750 ml of the drink. Now what will be the ratio of water to apple juice
   in the diluted drink? [Bapex- (AM)-2017-(Written)]+[Jamuna Bank Ltd.(MTO/PO)-2019]
                                                                                   Ans. 5:9
8. In a mixture 60 liters, the ratio of milk and water 2 : 1. If the ratio is to be 1 : 2, then
   estimate the quantity of water in liter to be further added in the mixture? [Standard
   Bank –( TAO-General)-2018]                                                    Ans. 60 liters
 Self Task:
9. The ratio of gold and silver in an ornament weighing 42 gram 4 : 3. How much gold will
   need to be added to make the ratio of gold and silver 5 : 3? [Janata, Sonali, Agrani Rupali
   Bank (SO) Examination -2008 (Written)] & [Bank Asia-2016-(written)]            Ans: 6 gm
