**(b)** Quarterly rate = 5% ÷ 4 = 1.25%; number of quarters = 12.

**(c)** x⁴ + 1/x⁴ = 119 ⇒ (x² + 1/x²)² − 2 = 119 ⇒ x² + 1/x² = 11.
(x − 1/x)² = x² + 1/x² − 2 = 9 ⇒ x − 1/x = 3 (taking the positive root).
x³ − 1/x³ = (x − 1/x)³ + 3(x − 1/x) = 27 + 9 = **36** (proved).

**(d)** (2x − 6)² = (x + 2)² + x² ⇒ 4x² − 24x + 36 = 2x² + 4x + 4 ⇒ x² − 14x + 16 = 0
x = 7 ± √33. Since the hypotenuse 2x − 6 must be positive, **x = 7 + √33 ≈ 12.74**. The data as printed give an irrational answer; check the original paper for a misprint.

## Ans. — January 2022 Mathematics

**(a)** Area of the two roads = 50 × 1.5 + 40 × 1.5 − 1.5 × 1.5 (the overlap is counted twice) = 75 + 60 − 2.25 = **132.75 m²**.

**(b)** Let the height be h and the first distance x. tan 60° = h/x ⇒ h = x√3; tan 45° = h/(x + 18) ⇒ h = x + 18.
x√3 = x + 18 ⇒ x = 18/(√3 − 1) = 9(√3 + 1)
h = 9(√3 + 1) + 18 = 27 + 9√3 ≈ **42.59 m**.

## Essay skeletons (old papers)

**50 Years of Bangladesh's Banking Management System: A Critical Evaluation (2022)**
Nationalisation (1972) → denationalisation and private banks (1980s) → Financial Sector Reform Programme (1990s) → Basel, digital banking, MFS (2010s) → crisis of governance and default loans (32.17%, Sept 2026) and the Bank Resolution Act 2026. *Achievements:* outreach, inclusion, MFS leadership, remittance channel. *Failures:* political lending, weak supervision, too many banks (about 60). *Way forward:* independent central bank, consolidation, fit-and-proper directors, ECL provisioning.

**The Role of the Banking Sector for Sustainable E-Commerce (2021)**
Payment gateways, Bangla QR, escrow services for consumer protection, SME credit for online sellers, cyber security, cross-border payments for freelancers, consumer trust after e-commerce scams.

**COP26 and Bangladesh's Partnership / Women's Struggle under Climate Change (2021–2022)**
Use the COP31 data box from the predicted climate essay. Add for women: salinity and drinking water, health, migration, unpaid care work; women-led adaptation and microfinance.

**Paper Recognition vs Real Economic Empowerment of Women (2023)**
Laws and policies exist; reality shows low female labour participation, the wage gap, limited land and asset ownership and few women in bank management; family card (Tk 2,500 a month) and women entrepreneurs' credit as positive steps; need childcare, safe transport and digital finance.

**Heatwave Reduction and Environmental Protection (2024)**
Urban heat islands, loss of trees and water bodies, heat stress for workers; responses: urban greening, wetland protection, heat action plans, green building codes, green finance.

**Modern Technology and Students' Academic Achievement (2024)**
Benefits: e-learning, access to information, AI tutors. Harms: distraction, social-media addiction, plagiarism. Balanced use, digital literacy and teacher training.

**Why you are fit to be a Senior Officer (2023 personal question)**
Structure: quantitative skills (numeracy for credit appraisal), English and Bangla proficiency (customer communication, reporting), cultural awareness (serving diverse customers), politeness (customer service and complaint handling), integrity and willingness to learn.
