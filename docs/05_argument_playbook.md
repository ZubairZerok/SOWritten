# ০৫ · আর্গুমেন্ট প্লেবুক (৪০ নম্বর)

## কাঠামো (প্রতিবার এটাই)

1. Introduction (3-4 lines): restate the claim + one fresh data point + your clear position (agree / disagree / partly agree).
2. Argument 1 + evidence (data / example from Bangladesh).
3. Argument 2 + evidence.
4. Argument 3 + evidence.
5. Counter-argument: 'Critics may argue that ... However, ...' — acknowledge, then rebut.
6. Recommendations / balanced way forward (3-4 bullet points).
7. Conclusion (2-3 lines) restating the position in new words.

**শব্দসীমা:** ৩৫০–৪৫০ শব্দ, সময় ২০–২২ মিনিট। প্রতিটি যুক্তির শুরুতে একটি **বোল্ড টপিক সেন্টেন্স** দিন, কারণ পরীক্ষক স্ক্যান করে পড়েন।

## অবস্থান বাছাইয়ের নিয়ম
- "only", "always", "best", "all" জাতীয় চরম দাবি এলে বেশিরভাগ ক্ষেত্রে **partly agree** নিন, কারণ এতে দুই পক্ষ দেখিয়ে বেশি নম্বর পাওয়া যায়।
- নীতিগত প্রশ্নে (যেমন subsidy, merger) **শর্তসাপেক্ষ সমর্থন** নিন: "Yes, but only if ..."
- অবস্থান ভূমিকাতেই স্পষ্ট লিখবেন, আর উপসংহারে তা থেকে সরবেন না।

## উপযোগী বাক্যাংশ

- It is often argued that ...
- At first glance, ... ; however, a closer look reveals ...
- This argument rests on the assumption that ...
- The evidence suggests otherwise.
- A case in point is ...
- Admittedly, ...; nevertheless, ...
- Critics may contend that ..., but ...
- It would be an oversimplification to claim that ...
- Taking all these factors into account, ...
- On balance, the claim is only partially valid.

## অনুশীলনের প্রম্পট (`so.py arg N --timer`)

### 1. "Raising the policy rate is the only effective way to control inflation in Bangladesh."
- **পক্ষে:** Higher rates curb demand and credit; Anchors expectations; global practice; Inflation fell from 11.66% (Jul 2024) to 8.27% (Oct 2026)
- **বিপক্ষে:** Inflation is largely cost-push (fuel, taka, syndicates); Non-food inflation reached 9.89% even with a 9.5–10% policy rate; Hurts investment: private credit growth only 5%
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Partly agree — needs supply-side + fiscal coordination.

### 2. "Merging weak banks with strong banks is the best solution to the banking crisis."
- **পক্ষে:** Protects depositors; Economies of scale; Sammilito Islami Bank example
- **বিপক্ষে:** Can infect strong banks; Rewards bad governance (moral hazard); Root causes (political lending) remain
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Merger only with AQR, accountability and fresh governance.

### 3. "Deferring LDC graduation to 2029 is a setback for Bangladesh."
- **পক্ষে:** Delays image boost and credit rating gains; May signal weakness
- **বিপক্ষে:** Keeps duty-free access (EBA) for 3 more years; Time to diversify exports and meet GSP+ conditions; Pharma TRIPS waiver continues
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Disagree — it is a strategic breathing space if used well.

### 4. "Fuel subsidies should be withdrawn completely."
- **পক্ষে:** Fiscal burden; BPC losses; Market pricing ensures efficiency; Reduces smuggling
- **বিপক্ষে:** Raises transport, irrigation and food costs; Hurts the poor; non-food inflation; Timing during high inflation
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Gradual, formula-based adjustment with targeted support.

### 5. "Artificial Intelligence will cause massive job losses in the banking sector."
- **পক্ষে:** Automation of routine tasks; Chatbots replace call centres
- **বিপক্ষে:** Creates new jobs (data, cyber, AI governance); Human judgement needed in credit and relations; Productivity gains expand services
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Jobs will change rather than vanish — reskilling is key.

### 6. "Only fraudulent business syndicates are responsible for the price hike of essential commodities."
- **পক্ষে:** Hoarding and market manipulation; Few importers control markets
- **বিপক্ষে:** Global prices, fuel, taka depreciation; Floods and crop loss; Weak market monitoring
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Partly responsible, not the only cause (sample answer in PDF48).

### 7. "Remittance, not exports, is the main driver of Bangladesh's economy."
- **পক্ষে:** Record inflows, near-balanced current account; Supports rural consumption
- **বিপক্ষে:** Exports (~$40bn+) earn more and create jobs; Remittance is volatile and consumption-oriented
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Both are pillars; exports drive productive capacity.

### 8. "A cashless society is not suitable for Bangladesh now."
- **পক্ষে:** Digital illiteracy; Cyber fraud; Rural internet gaps
- **বিপক্ষে:** MFS penetration is high; Bangla QR and IIPS make it interoperable; Reduces corruption and costs
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** A gradual 'less-cash' path, not cash-free overnight.

### 9. "Not consumers, manufacturers are mainly responsible for environmental pollution."
- **পক্ষে:** Industrial effluents, ETP non-use; Brick kilns
- **বিপক্ষে:** Consumer plastic use and demand; Weak enforcement by government
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Shared responsibility; producers bear larger share (8 Bank SO question).

### 10. "Government borrowing from banks is crowding out the private sector."
- **পক্ষে:** Public credit growth 25.9% vs private 5.5%; Banks prefer risk-free T-bills
- **বিপক্ষে:** Low private demand is due to uncertainty, high rates; Excess liquidity of Tk 3.28 lakh crore exists
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Partly true — fix revenue mobilisation and investment climate.

### 11. "Social media does more harm than good to the youth."
- **পক্ষে:** Addiction, misinformation, mental health
- **বিপক্ষে:** Freelancing, learning, civic voice
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Regulated and literate use maximises benefits.

### 12. "Public universities should prioritise job-oriented education over research."
- **পক্ষে:** Graduate unemployment; Skill mismatch
- **বিপক্ষে:** Research drives innovation; Narrow training becomes obsolete
- **সম্ভাব্য অবস্থান:** Balance — industry linkage plus research funding.
