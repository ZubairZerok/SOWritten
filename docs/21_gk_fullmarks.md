## 4. Bangladesh: constitution, symbols, geography, culture

### 4.1 Constitution and state

- The Constitution has **153 articles** and was drafted by a committee headed by **Dr Kamal Hossain**.
- **Fundamental principles of state policy:** Part II (Articles 8–25). **Fundamental rights:** Part III (Articles 26–47).
- **Parliament:** the Jatiya Sangsad (unicameral), with 300 elected seats plus 50 reserved for women. The building was designed by **Louis I. Kahn**.
- **Supreme Court:** an Appellate Division and a High Court Division.
- **Constitutional bodies:** the Election Commission, the Public Service Commission, the Comptroller and Auditor General and the Attorney General.

### 4.4 Mega-projects and firsts

- **Padma Bridge:** 6.15 km, opened **25 Jun 2022**, self-financed.
- **Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line-6):** opened 28 Dec 2022.
- **Karnaphuli tunnel:** Chattogram, opened 28 Oct 2023. It is South Asia's first under-river road tunnel.
- **Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant:** Pabna, 2,400 MW, built with Russia. Bangladesh is the 33rd nuclear-power country.
- **Matarbari deep-sea port:** Cox's Bazar, with Japan's JICA, about 18.5 m deep.
- **First communication satellite:** launched 12 May 2018, making Bangladesh the 57th country with its own satellite.
- **Nobel laureates linked to Bengal:** Rabindranath Tagore (Literature 1913), Amartya Sen (Economics 1998), Muhammad Yunus and Grameen Bank (Peace 2006).

### 4.5 Culture and heritage

- **UNESCO World Heritage Sites:** the Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat (1985), the Ruins of the Buddhist Vihara at Paharpur (1985) and the Sundarbans (1997).
- **UNESCO intangible heritage:** Baul songs (2008), Jamdani weaving (2013), Mangal Shobhajatra (2016), Shitalpati weaving (2017) and Dhaka's rickshaw painting (2023).
- **First Geographical Indication (GI) product:** **Jamdani** (2016). Hilsa was the second.
- **Literary firsts:** the first Bangla novel is *Alaler Gharer Dulal* by Peary Chand Mitra. The first modern Bangla epic is *Meghnad Badh Kabya* by Michael Madhusudan Dutta. The oldest Bangla literary work is the *Charyapada*.

## 14. Three timed mocks (10 minutes each, 5 × 2)

### Mock A

1. Where is the permanent secretariat of BIMSTEC?
2. Which strait links the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman?
3. Who publishes the Human Development Report?
4. In which year did Bangladesh get Test status in cricket?
5. Where is COP31 being held?

**Key A.**

1. The permanent secretariat of BIMSTEC is in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**.
2. The **Strait of Hormuz** links them. It is a chokepoint for about a fifth of world oil and has been effectively closed since Feb 2026.
3. The **United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)** publishes it, including the HDI.
4. Bangladesh became a Test-playing nation in **2000** (the 10th).
5. COP31 is in **Antalya, Türkiye** (9–20 November 2026).

### Mock B

1. What is the Radcliffe Line?
2. Which UN agency is headquartered in Paris?
3. When did UNESCO declare 21 February International Mother Language Day?
4. Who is the President of the 81st UN General Assembly?
5. Which country hosts the 2026 Asian Games?

**Key B.**

1. The **Radcliffe Line** is the boundary drawn by Sir Cyril Radcliffe in 1947 that partitioned Bengal and Punjab between India and Pakistan.
2. **UNESCO** (UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) is headquartered in Paris.
3. On **17 November 1999**; it has been observed worldwide since 2000.
4. **Khalilur Rahman** of Bangladesh. He is the second Bangladeshi after Humayun Rasheed Choudhury (1986).
5. **Japan** (Aichi–Nagoya, 2026).

### Mock C

1. Which is the largest landlocked country?
2. What is "swing state"?
3. Which country is the world's first to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender?
4. What is Swatch of No Ground?
5. To when has Bangladesh's LDC graduation been deferred?

**Key C.**

1. **Kazakhstan** is the world's largest landlocked country.
2. A **swing state** is a US state where support for the two major parties is close, so it can decide a presidential election.
3. **El Salvador** (2021).
4. A deep **submarine canyon in the Bay of Bengal**, south of the Sundarbans. It is a marine protected area rich in dolphins and whales.
5. To **24 November 2029**, under UN General Assembly resolution A/81/L.8 (29 October 2026).
